# A practical way to earn from this project

Start with a free fan community. This source package has no payment system or betting features. A website by itself does not guarantee visitors or revenue.

## Best fit for your existing skills

You already make graphics, motion content and AI ads. Use those skills to attract cricket fans and sell sponsor creative work alongside a free league.

1. Invite an initial pilot group from Lahore cricket clubs, friends and cricket communities where promotion is allowed. Aim for 30–100 genuine testers, not purchased traffic. Give each club its own private league code.
2. Make short reels showing the mobile player picker and explain one selection or transfer decision. End with the free league invitation. Use original footage/graphics or licensed material.
3. Run a small practice competition, verify results, and measure repeat participation, weekly active players and team-save completion. Fix complaints before promising a large event.
4. Approach local sports shops, cricket academies and cafes with real participation numbers. Offer a sponsored league plus one poster and two reels. Your design/video work provides value even while the site audience is small. Pricing must be negotiated; no revenue estimate is guaranteed here.
5. Sell optional club services: setting up their league, creating weekly standings posters, short highlight graphics and campaign creatives. The current app already provides private league codes; it does not automatically charge for them.
6. Later consider tracked affiliate links for legitimate cricket equipment, with clear disclosure and a Pakistan-compatible programme you have verified. No affiliate programme or payout account is connected by this code.
7. Consider display advertising only after adding useful original public content and building repeat visits. AdSense lists Pakistan among supported locations, but site approval is separate. Google requires quality original content and an audience; the existence of a team picker alone does not assure approval.

## What to avoid in the first launch

Do not budget around guaranteed ad income. Do not buy fake clicks, ask users to click ads, place ads over player controls, or present unverified live points as real. Keep the selection flow fast and clean. Advertisements, if added, belong on content/overview pages, not over the captain/save buttons.

Do not add entry fees, deposits or cash prizes casually. Those would require a separate product, payment and jurisdiction-specific legal review; they are outside this free-to-play build.

## Your first outreach offer

Offer a free private league to a local cricket academy. Show the working mobile flow and offer to design its branded weekly leaderboard poster and two promotional reels for an agreed fee. This is a more concrete early service offer than relying only on ad revenue from an unknown audience.
